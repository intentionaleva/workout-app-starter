# Eva's plan (example)

This is my personal plan for my husband and me. It's here so the app works right after you fork it,
and so you have a complete, real-world example to copy from. **Replace it with your own.**

- **Morgenroutine / Morning routine** (daily, ~8 min): glute bridges, knee wipers and reverse crunches in bed, then mobility on the mat.
- **Training A / B** (2× a week, ~35 min): kettlebell and dumbbell strength, with a shared warm-up and core block.
- **Ausdauer / Cardio**: tick off 30 minutes, 5× a week (150 min, the WHO recommendation).
- **Rückbildung / Postpartum** (15 min): a gentle circuit for after pregnancy. Please read [DISCLAIMER.md](../../DISCLAIMER.md) first.

These files are an untouched copy of `config/` as shipped, so you can always come back to them.
